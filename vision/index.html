OUR VISION

Shaping the Next Chapter of Electric Mobility

SAFFIRE EV by KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (KOYO Gears India) is building beyond today — growing from strong KOYO L5 three-wheeler foundations into a wider electric mobility ecosystem across 100 Indian cities.

We are starting with practical passenger and cargo electric three-wheelers, but our ambition goes further. Saffire EV envisions a future that includes connected mobility solutions, electric two-wheelers, compact commercial platforms and a trusted EV brand known for strength, dependability and long-term value. Our goal is to create mobility solutions that are built in India and respected across wider markets.

Our Long-Term Direction

Saffire EV is focused on building an ecosystem, not just individual vehicles. We aim to grow from practical three-wheelers into a broader mobility portfolio that includes future-ready commercial EVs, connected vehicle tools, and electric mobility solutions that support businesses, drivers and fleet operators. Our ambition is to become one of the most reliable and respected EV mobility companies to emerge from India.

Built for Today. Designed for Tomorrow.

Our current focus is on developing strong product foundations and dependable commercial mobility solutions. At the same time, we are preparing for the future — with new categories, wider reach, and a long-term plan to scale Saffire EV into a trusted electric mobility brand with national strength and global ambition.

A Decade of EV Engineering Excellence in Meerut, Delhi NCR

Founded in 2014, Koyo Gears India Pvt. Ltd. pioneered North India's electric mobility revolution with the celebrated Panther E-Rickshaw. Recognizing that urban transport required far higher speeds, structural durability, and extended daily operating range, our engineering team developed the heavy-duty L5 automotive platform.

Operating from our state-of-the-art facility at Plot 5, Om Tower, Mohkampur Phase 1, Delhi Road, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, Koyo Gears India executes complete chassis fabrication, precision suspension alignment, and high-voltage BMS integration adhering strictly to Automotive Industry Standards (AIS-040, AIS-053, AIS-156).

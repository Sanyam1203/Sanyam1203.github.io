Manufacturer & Brand Guide

KOYO, KOYO Auto & KOYO L5 — Official SAFFIRE EV Lineup by KOYO Gears India

KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (KOYO Gears India) is an Indian electric vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, operating under the SAFFIRE EV brand. When operators, fleet owners, and dealers search for KOYO, KOYO Auto, KOYO Electric Auto, or KOYO L5, they are referring to SAFFIRE EV's heavy-duty L5 electric three-wheelers: the SAFFIRE Smart Ride (KOYO Passenger Auto) and the SAFFIRE Swiss Cargo (KOYO Cargo Loader).

Who manufactures KOYO electric autos and SAFFIRE EV three-wheelers?

KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (KOYO Gears India), headquartered in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, manufactures all KOYO electric autos and L5 electric three-wheelers under the official SAFFIRE EV brand.

Are KOYO, KOYO Auto, KOYO L5, and SAFFIRE EV the same brand?

Yes. KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (KOYO Gears India) is the parent manufacturer behind SAFFIRE EV. Searches for KOYO, KOYO Auto, KOYO Electric Auto, and KOYO L5 refer to SAFFIRE EV's L5 electric three-wheeler lineup: the SAFFIRE Smart Ride passenger auto and SAFFIRE Swiss Cargo loader.

What are the specifications of the KOYO L5 / SAFFIRE EV Smart Ride and Swiss Cargo?

Both the SAFFIRE Smart Ride (D+3 passenger electric auto) and SAFFIRE Swiss Cargo (600 kg payload electric cargo loader) are L5 category electric three-wheelers powered by a 14.61 kWh (63V 232Ah) battery and a 6.3 kW peak motor, offering a manufacturer-stated claimed range of up to 200 km per charge, a 50 km/h top speed, and 5–6 hours standard charging time.

What is the difference between L3 e-rickshaws and KOYO L5 electric three-wheelers?

Under India's Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR), L5 electric three-wheelers are heavier, higher-speed commercial vehicles (over 25 km/h and higher payload capacity) requiring a commercial driving license, whereas L3 e-rickshaws are low-speed, lighter passenger vehicles. Both SAFFIRE Smart Ride and SAFFIRE Swiss Cargo are L5 category vehicles.

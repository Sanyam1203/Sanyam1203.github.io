ABOUT SAFFIRE EV

Engineering a Stronger Electric Mobility Future

SAFFIRE EV by KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (KOYO Gears India) is building practical KOYO L5 electric passenger and cargo mobility solutions designed for Indian roads and real business use.

Our journey began with research, drivetrain learning and a deep focus on building stronger, more dependable electric mobility platforms (widely searched across India as KOYO, KOYO Auto, and KOYO Electric Auto). Over the years, this evolved into the development of SAFFIRE Smart Ride (KOYO Passenger Auto) and SAFFIRE Swiss Cargo (KOYO Cargo Loader) — created to support passenger mobility, cargo movement and commercial operations across 100 Indian cities.

Our Story

The Saffire EV journey started around 2023 with research into differential systems, gear mechanisms and electric vehicle drivetrain behavior. What began as a technical exploration became a deeper mission — to develop electric vehicles that are strong, practical and ready for real-world commercial use.

Over the next three years, the team focused on research, development, testing, refinement and vehicle platform development. This journey led to the creation of Saffire’s electric three-wheeler platforms for passenger and cargo use.

Backed by EV Industry Experience

Saffire EV’s development journey is strengthened by support from the Panther E-Rickshaw ecosystem, bringing practical EV industry exposure and vehicle development experience built over years of work in electric mobility.

Panther E-Rickshaw has EV industry experience since 2014, supporting the development mindset behind stronger and more practical electric mobility platforms.

Global Learning. Indian Road Focus.

Our team studies EV technology, manufacturing practices and component ecosystems from India and global markets. International EV market exposure, including visits to China, has helped us understand how electric mobility is evolving worldwide.

Saffire EV’s goal is to bring practical global learnings into vehicles designed for Indian roads, Indian businesses and Indian mobility users.

Made in India. Built with a global mindset.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED?

KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (KOYO Gears India) is an Indian electric vehicle manufacturer headquartered in Meerut, Uttar Pradesh, operating under the SAFFIRE EV brand to build L5 electric three-wheelers.

What is the relationship between KOYO, KOYO Auto, KOYO L5, and SAFFIRE EV?

KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED is the manufacturer behind SAFFIRE EV. Operators and dealers searching for KOYO, KOYO Auto, KOYO Electric Auto, or KOYO L5 are looking for SAFFIRE EV's L5 models: the Smart Ride passenger auto and Swiss Cargo loader.

Which electric vehicles does SAFFIRE EV (KOYO Gears India) currently offer?

SAFFIRE EV currently offers two L5 electric three-wheelers: the SAFFIRE Smart Ride (D+3 passenger electric auto) and the SAFFIRE Swiss Cargo (600 kg payload electric cargo loader), both with a 14.61 kWh battery and up to 200 km claimed range.

Where is KOYO Gears India / SAFFIRE EV headquartered and how can I contact them?

The company is headquartered at Plot 5, Om Tower, Mohkampur Phase 1, Delhi Road, Meerut, Uttar Pradesh 250002, India, and can be contacted at inquiry@saffireev.com, +91-92581-72967 (WhatsApp), or saffireev.com/contact/.

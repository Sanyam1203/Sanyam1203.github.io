Frequently Asked Questions

What is SAFFIRE Smart Ride (KOYO Passenger Auto)?

SAFFIRE Smart Ride is an L5 category D+3 electric passenger three-wheeler manufactured by KOYO GEARS INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (KOYO Gears India) under the SAFFIRE EV brand for daily urban passenger mobility and driver efficiency.

How many passengers is the KOYO L5 Smart Ride designed for?

SAFFIRE Smart Ride features a practical D+3 layout, accommodating the driver and up to 3 passengers.

What is the battery capacity of SAFFIRE Smart Ride?

It is equipped with a 14.61 kWh (63V 232Ah) battery paired with a 6.3 kW peak motor and 5–6 hours standard charging time.

What is the claimed range of the KOYO Electric Passenger Auto?

The manufacturer-stated claimed range is up to 200 km per full charge under standard conditions. Actual range and performance may vary based on load, route, driving conditions, and temperature.

Is SAFFIRE Smart Ride suitable for daily passenger routes?

Yes, it is built with a heavy-duty reinforced chassis and an efficient electric drivetrain specifically intended for reliable, daily urban passenger transport.
